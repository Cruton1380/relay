## 📦 WHAT IS AUDIT IN RELAY?

**Audit answers 6 questions:**

1. **Existence:** Does this object/event exist in Relay?
2. **Completeness:** Are all material objects/events represented?
3. **Accuracy:** Does the representation match reality?
4. **Consistency:** Do equivalent realities have equivalent representations?
5. **Temporal Integrity:** Is history preserved (no retroactive edits)?
6. **Traceability:** Can we trace every state to its source?

**Audit does NOT:**
- Create truth (only verifies)
- Punish violations (only records findings)
- Centralize authority (anyone can audit)
- Produce global scores (findings are scoped)

## 🔑 THE 6 UNIVERSAL AUDIT OBJECTS

### **1. MaterialityRule**
**Defines when real-world reality MUST exist in Relay.**

**Examples:**
- Financial: Transactions > $10k must be logged
- Military: Strategic assets must exist as ForceUnits
- Infrastructure: Buildings serving >10k people must exist

### **2. RealityCoverageMap**
**Maps real-world domains → Relay representations. Shows gaps.**

**Metrics:**
- Coverage percentage (0.0-1.0)
- Coverage quality (Complete / Partial / Incomplete)
- Critical gaps count
- Gaps by domain

### **3. AuditAssertion**
**Claims that must be verified.**

**Types:**
- Existence
- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Rights & Permissions
- Valuation / Magnitude
- Temporal Correctness
- Consistency

### **4. AuditEvidence**
**Filament-linked artifacts proving assertions.**

**Types:**
- Sensor data
- Filament commits
- External attestations
- Physical inspections
- Cryptographic proofs
- Consensus votes

### **5. AuditFinding**
**Mismatch, omission, inconsistency, or unverifiable state.**

**Types:**
- MissingObject
- InaccurateState
- InconsistentRepresentation
- TemporalViolation
- UnverifiableAssertion
- IndependenceViolation
- MaterialityViolation

### **6. AuditOpinion**
**Scoped conclusion (not global).**

**Types:**
- Verified (clean opinion)
- VerifiedWithExceptions (qualified)
- Incomplete (scope limitation)
- NotVerifiable (disclaimer)
- Adverse (material misstatement)

### **Step 3: Generate Coverage Map**

- Focus on Critical/High gaps

### **Step 4: Test Assertions**

- Verify evidence requirements met

### **Step 5: Collect Evidence**
- Review AuditEvidence objects
- Verify independence, recency, authenticity

### **Step 6: Review Findings**
- Check existing findings
- Create new findings as needed

### **Step 7: Issue Opinion**
- Scoped to domain + zone + period
- Verified / VerifiedWithExceptions / Incomplete / Adverse
