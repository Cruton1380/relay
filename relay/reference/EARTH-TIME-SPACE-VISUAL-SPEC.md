# EARTH-TIME-SPACE VISUAL SPECIFICATION

**Image Generation Prompt & Design Rules**  
**Type:** Visual Reference  
**Purpose:** Generate canonical Earth-Time-Space model visualization  
**Date:** 2026-01-29

---

## 🎨 THE IMAGE CONCEPT

**What the image must show:**

1. **Earth as a transparent time volume**
2. **Concentric shells inside (day-layers)**
3. **Active filaments on surface (now)**
4. **Drill bore-path visible (history navigation)**
5. **Space objects outside (unconnected filaments)**
6. **Clean forensic aesthetic (not fantasy)**

---

## 📐 VISUAL STRUCTURE

### **Core Elements:**

```
        ← SPACE (Observable/Distant) →
              ╱            ╲
         Star/Planet    Star/Planet
              ↑            ↑
              │            │
    ═════════════════════════════════
    ║      SURFACE (Now)            ║  ← Active coordination
    ║  Buildings | Filaments | SCVs ║
    ═════════════════════════════════
    ║    CRUST (Recent History)     ║  ← Days/weeks
    ║────────────────────────────────║
    ║   MANTLE (Older History)      ║  ← Months/years
    ║────────────────────────────────║
    ║     CORE (Genesis)            ║  ← System roots
    ═════════════════════════════════
              ↓            ↓
        History Drill   History Drill
```

## 🌍 EARTH RENDERING

### **1. Globe Structure:**

**Material:** Translucent, not opaque  
**Surface:** Faint continents visible  
**Atmosphere:** Thin blue glow  
**Aesthetic:** Matte, forensic, not glossy

---

### **2. Internal Shells (Concentric Layers):**

**Visual:** Tree rings, but for coordination  
**Count:** Show 5-7 visible layers  
**Spacing:** Closer near surface, wider deeper  
**Color coding:**
- Surface: Bright (white/blue glow)
- Crust: Lighter gray (recent)
- Mantle: Darker gray (older)
- Core: Dense gold/amber (genesis)

**Texture:** Subtle radial striations (not solid fills)

---

### **3. Surface Activity (Current):**

**Show on Earth's surface:**
- Small glowing buildings (capability anchors)
- Thin luminous filaments (arcs between points)
- Small moving units (drones/SCVs)
- Faint zone boundaries (regional divisions)

**Colors:**
- Buildings: Matte concrete gray with subtle glow
- Filaments: Blue (#4A9EFF), thin, luminous
- Units: Small dots, varied colors by type
- Zones: Faint white lines

**Density:** Moderate (not cluttered, not empty)

---

## 📏 DRILL BORE-PATH

### **What to Show:**

**Visual:** Vertical shaft from surface to core

**Key features:**
- Origin point on surface (highlighted building)
- Straight line downward
- Cuts through all shells
- Visible cross-sections
- Ends at core

**Rendering:**
- Path: Glowing line (yellow/white)
- Cross-sections: Highlighted where it intersects shells
- Width: Thin, precise (like a laser)
- Transparency: Can see through to other side

**Purpose:** Shows "drilling down into history"

---

## 🌌 SPACE OBJECTS (OUTSIDE)

### **What to Show:**

**Objects in space around Earth:**
- 3-5 distant planets/stars
- Varying distances
- Different sizes (perspective)
- Some connected, some unconnected

**Rendering:**

**1. Connected Objects (With Filament)**
- Faint blue line from Earth → Object
- Object has slight glow
- Represents "we know about this and have data"

**2. Unconnected Objects (No Filament)**
- No line to Earth
- Dimmer glow
- Represents "we can see it but haven't reached it"

**3. Distance Shells (Optional)**
- Faint concentric spheres around Earth
- Represent distance scales
- Very subtle (not dominant)

**Colors:**
- Planets: Varied (Earth-like, gas giant colors)
- Stars: Yellow/white glow
- Filaments: Same blue as surface (#4A9EFF)

---

## 🎨 COLOR PALETTE (EXACT)

### **Primary Colors:**

**Earth:**
- Surface glow: `#4A9EFF` (blue)
- Continents: `#666666` (gray)
- Atmosphere: `#87CEEB` (sky blue, transparent)

**Shells:**
- Surface: `#E0E0E0` (light gray, glowing)
- Crust: `#B0B0B0` (medium gray)
- Mantle: `#808080` (darker gray)
- Core: `#FFD700` (gold, dense)

**Surface Activity:**
- Buildings: `#8B8B8B` (concrete gray)
- Filaments: `#4A9EFF` (blue, luminous)
- Units: `#FFD700` (gold), `#4A9EFF` (blue), `#8B8B8B` (gray)

**Drill Path:**
- Line: `#FFD700` (gold) or `#FFFFFF` (white)
- Glow: `#FFEB3B` (yellow highlight)

**Space:**
- Background: `#0A0A0A` (near-black)
- Stars: `#FFFFFF` (white dots, distant)
- Planets: Earth tones, blues, oranges
- Filaments: `#4A9EFF` (same as Earth)

---

## 🚫 WHAT TO AVOID

**Do NOT include:**
- ❌ Fantasy vines/organic growths
- ❌ Colorful aurora effects (too decorative)
- ❌ Dense web of nodes (ambiguous)
- ❌ Glossy/shiny materials
- ❌ Dramatic lighting (keep neutral)
- ❌ Abstract symbols (stay literal)
- ❌ Text labels (pure visual)

---

## 📋 RENDERING SPECIFICATIONS

### **Technical Details:**

**Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160) minimum  
**Format:** PNG with alpha channel  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 1:1 (square)  
**Lighting:** Even, ambient (forensic quality)  
**Depth of Field:** None (everything sharp)  
**Anti-aliasing:** High quality  
**Transparency:** Use for shells and atmosphere
