## 🏗️ RELAY-BASED ARCHITECTURE

**All coordination MUST use only Relay primitives:**

### **Zone Structure:**

#### **1. Planetary Observation Zone**
**Purpose:** Detect and catalog anomalous phenomena

**Rules:**
- Scientific instruments only (no weapons)
- Data publicly available (default transparency)
- Interpretation separated from detection
- False-positive protocols explicit
- No panic alerts without verification

**Objects:**
- `ObservationStation` (telescopes, sensors)
- `AnomalyReport` (structured observations)
- `VerificationProtocol` (multi-site confirmation)
- `SignalInterpretation` (provisional, marked as uncertain)

---

#### **2. Scientific Interpretation Zone**
**Purpose:** Analyze data without militarization

**Rules:**
- Expert participation voluntary
- All interpretations provisional
- Uncertainty explicit
- Multiple competing hypotheses preserved
- No "consensus" forced prematurely

**Objects:**
- `InterpretationBranch` (competing theories)
- `EvidenceArtifact` (data supporting each branch)
- `UncertaintyMarker` (what we don't know)
- `ExpertAttestation` (identity + reasoning)

---

#### **3. Public Communication Zone**
**Purpose:** Inform without panic

**Rules:**
- Headers first (zoom to proof optional)
- Uncertainty always visible
- Multiple interpretations shown
- No "official narrative"
- Rumor vs evidence clearly marked

**Objects:**
- `PublicBriefing` (Layer 0 + Layer 1 structure)
- `InterpretationCompeting` (show all theories)
- `EvidenceLink` (trace to source data)
- `MisinformationMarker` (debunked claims)

---

#### **4. Coordination Zone**
**Purpose:** Organize response without centralization

**Rules:**
- Delegated, revocable influence
- Time-bounded authority
- Scope-limited decisions
- No permanent leaders
- Audit all decisions

**Objects:**
- `CoordinationTask` (specific, bounded)
- `AuthorityGrant` (temporary, scoped, decaying)
- `DecisionCommit` (append-only, traceable)
- `DisputeProcess` (handle disagreements)

---

#### **5. Infrastructure Resilience Zone**
**Purpose:** Protect continuity without militarization

**Rules:**
- Redundancy over concentration
- Decentralization over central control
- Hardening over weapons
- Fail-safe over fail-deadly
- Civil defense, not military

**Objects:**
- `CriticalInfrastructure` (energy, food, water, comms)
- `RedundancyProtocol` (fallback systems)
- `HardeningMeasure` (physical + cyber resilience)
- `FailSafeGate` (automatic shutdowns if needed)

---

#### **6. Last-Resort Defense Zone (HEAVILY CONSTRAINED)**
**Purpose:** Protective containment only if all else fails

**Rules:**
- Activated only if:
  - Diplomacy exhausted
  - Clear immediate threat to life
  - No other options remain
  - Human authorization required
  - Time-limited (auto-expires)
  - Geographically bounded
  - Audited in real-time

- NO preemptive strikes
- NO weapons development as part of this scenario
- NO tactical optimization
- NO global military command

**Objects:**
- `DefensivePerimeter` (zone-bounded)
- `ThreatContainment` (minimize harm, not maximize damage)
- `AuthorizationCommit` (human decision, traceable)
- `AutoExpiry` (authority automatically revokes)

**This zone violates Relay principles if used offensively.**

## 🧠 COGNITIVE LOAD & HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

### **Prevent Panic:**

**Design:**
- SCVs analyze data, humans decide
- Enforced pause mechanisms (no immediate action required)
- "Unknown acknowledged" states (it's okay not to know)
- Multiple competing theories shown (no forced consensus)

**Objects:**
- `CognitiveLoadDistributor` (distribute analysis to many SCVs)
- `PauseMechanism` (force waiting period before major decisions)
- `UncertaintyState` (explicit "we don't know" marker)
- `CompetingTheories` (preserve disagreement)

---

### **Prevent Obsession / Fixation:**

**Design:**
- Attention rotation (no one person monitors 24/7)
- Mandatory rest periods
- Cognitive load limits
- Diverse perspective requirements

**Objects:**
- `AttentionRotation` (shift monitoring duty)
- `CognitiveCapacityGate` (prevent burnout)
- `DiversityRequirement` (multiple backgrounds required)

---

### **Prevent Cult Authority:**

**Design:**
- No single "expert" becomes oracle
- All interpretations provisional
- Authority decays automatically
- Disagreement encouraged

**Objects:**
- `AuthorityDecay` (influence erodes over time)
- `ExpertRotation` (no permanent advisors)
- `DisagreementReward` (challenging consensus is valued)

---

### **Prevent Militarization Drift:**

**Design:**
- Defense = resilience, not combat
- No weapons optimization
- Civil protection only
- Audit triggers if military language appears

**Objects:**
- `MilitarizationDetector` (flags combat-oriented language)
- `CivilDefenseGate` (enforce non-military framing)
- `AuditTrigger` (investigate if red flags appear)

## ⚠️ FAILURE MODES (REQUIRED ANALYSIS)

**For each failure mode:**
- Detection mechanism
- Mitigation strategy
- Recovery protocol

---

### **1. Panic Cascade**

**Description:**
- Initial signal → media frenzy → public panic → irrational decisions

**Detection:**
- Spike in emotional language
- Misinformation velocity increase
- Abandonment of reasoning

**Mitigation:**
- Public briefings (headers + proof structure)
- Competing interpretations shown
- Uncertainty explicit
- Expert attestations visible

**Recovery:**
- Trace panic origin
- Counter with evidence
- Slow down decision-making

---

### **2. Authoritarian Capture**

**Description:**
- Emergency → strong leader emerges → refuses to relinquish power

**Detection:**
- Authority grants not decaying
- Scope creep (authority expanding beyond original bounds)
- Audit evasion

**Mitigation:**
- Time-bounded authority (auto-expires)
- Scope-limited decisions
- Real-time audit
- Revocation mechanisms

**Recovery:**
- Force expiry of authority
- Redistribute influence
- Audit all decisions made

---

### **3. Religious Reinterpretation**

**Description:**
- Signal interpreted as divine/apocalyptic → cult formation

**Detection:**
- Religious language spike
- Abandonment of evidence-based reasoning
- Authority claimed through revelation

**Mitigation:**
- Scientific interpretation zones separate
- Multiple theories preserved
- Evidence links required
- No "official" interpretation

**Recovery:**
- Show competing secular explanations
- Trace evidence chains
- Audit theological claims

---

### **4. Militarization Drift**

**Description:**
- Fear → weapons development → preemptive strike → escalation

**Detection:**
- Combat language
- Weapons optimization attempts
- Preemptive strike proposals
- Global command structure proposals

**Mitigation:**
- Civil defense framing enforced
- MilitarizationDetector active
- Audit triggers
- No global military command allowed

**Recovery:**
- Shut down military proposals
- Redirect to civil resilience
- Audit decision-makers

---

### **5. Information Overload**

**Description:**
- Too much data → paralysis → inability to decide

**Detection:**
- Decision latency increasing
- Cognitive load exceeding capacity
- Expert burnout

**Mitigation:**
- SCVs distribute analysis
- Headers-first communication
- Cognitive load gates
- Attention rotation

**Recovery:**
- Simplify to essential decisions
- Delegate more to SCVs
- Reduce information flow

---

### **6. False-Positive Contact**

**Description:**
- Misinterpretation of natural phenomenon as intelligent signal

**Detection:**
- Single-source observation
- Lack of multi-site confirmation
- Pattern-matching bias

**Mitigation:**
- Multi-site verification required
- Competing hypotheses preserved
- Null hypothesis explicit
- Uncertainty markers

**Recovery:**
- Trace verification failures
- Adjust detection protocols
- Learn from false alarm
